# Professional services

RepoSource Registry remains open-source infrastructure. Professional services are a separate commercial layer for teams that need engineering expertise, customization, implementation, research, or support around the public dataset.

## Data integration

Integrate the public registry into:

- internal developer tools;
- developer portals and catalogs;
- research pipelines;
- AI/data workflows;
- recommendation or discovery applications.

## Custom data engineering

Build organization-specific processing pipelines, filters, transformations, derived views, or repeatable analysis workflows using the public registry and other permitted inputs.

## AI / RAG integration

Design workflows that consume structured repository metadata in retrieval, classification, recommendation, or analysis systems. The service focuses on implementation and architecture; the public dataset remains publicly available.

## Repository intelligence

Build specialized analysis workflows for repository popularity, language distribution, topic discovery, ecosystem structure, change analysis, or other questions supported by the available data.

## Research support

Produce custom ecosystem studies, analytical reports, reproducible research workflows, or specialized derived datasets where the required source data and methodology are appropriate.

## Open-source engineering

Provide implementation support for teams building tools on top of RepoSource Registry, including integration design, automation, testing, and maintainability improvements.

## Commercial boundary

Customers pay for:

- engineering time;
- implementation;
- customization;
- architecture;
- specialized analysis;
- research work;
- support.

They do not purchase ownership of the canonical public registry. Public collection and classification rules remain independent of customer or sponsor identity.

## No artificial scarcity

The project will not make the public dataset worse, hide canonical records, or restrict basic dataset availability solely to create a sales opportunity. Commercial services should create value through work performed around the open data.

## Engagement model

Potential engagements should begin with a defined problem, available inputs, expected deliverable, validation method, and maintenance responsibility. Pricing should be determined by scope and engineering effort rather than by access to the public dataset.
