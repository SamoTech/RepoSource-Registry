# Research partnerships

RepoSource Registry can support collaborative research where the published snapshot is an explicit, reproducible input.

## Collaboration models

- Open-source ecosystem analysis
- Repository metadata quality studies
- Reproducibility and snapshot methodology research
- Developer-tooling experiments
- Public research notes and derived datasets

## Proposed engagement

Define:

1. research question;
2. exact snapshot or release;
3. methodology;
4. expected public artifact;
5. limitations;
6. attribution and citation.

Potential outputs include an analysis notebook, public report, reproducible example, validation improvement, or documentation update.

## Independence

A research relationship does not change repository inclusion, ranking, classification, or collection rules. The public registry remains governed by its documented methodology.

## No invented partnerships

This page describes an available collaboration model. It is not evidence of a current academic, institutional, corporate, or research partnership.
